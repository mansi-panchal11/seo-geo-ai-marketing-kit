## Voice in three words

Clear, practical, confident.

## Rules

| Topic | Do | Do not |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Write in direct sentences and address the reader as you | Use slogans or empty superlatives |
| Claims | State a figure and its source | Use words such as best, leading or revolutionary without proof |
| Vocabulary | Use the terms your buyers use | Use internal jargon or acronyms without explanation |
| Structure | Put the answer first, then the reasoning | Bury the point at the end |
| Calls to action | Offer one clear next step | Offer several competing actions |
| Names | Use the official product and company names | Invent new names or abbreviations |
