# Expert interview guide

A 20 to 30 minute conversation is enough. Record it only with the expert's consent.

## Before the interview

1. Share the topic and the three to five buyer questions the article should answer.
2. Ask the expert to bring one real example, one figure and one common mistake.

## Questions

1. Who asks you about this topic, and what do they usually want to know first?
2. What is the short answer you would give in one minute?
3. What are the three most important facts or figures someone should remember?
4. Where do customers most often get this wrong?
5. What does a good result look like, with a real example?
6. What has changed in the last two years?
7. What would you compare this with, and when is the alternative a better choice?
8. What should a buyer ask a supplier before deciding?
9. Which of your statements need a source, and where can I find it?
10. What must we not say because it is not proven or not approved?

## After the interview

- Run the extraction prompt in `prompts/01-extract-claims.md`.
- Send the claims register to the expert for validation.
- Do not publish any claim the expert has not confirmed.
