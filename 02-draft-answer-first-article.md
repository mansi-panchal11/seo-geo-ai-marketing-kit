# Prompt 2. Draft an answer-first article

**Used in** step 5, draft answer-first content
**Human check** an editor reviews facts, tone and structure before approval

## Prompt

```text
You are a B2B content writer. Write an article that answers one buyer question.

Target question
[paste the prompt from your prompt set]

Audience
[persona and role]

Validated facts you may use
[paste only the validated rows from the claims register]

Structure
- Title that matches the question and does not start with the brand name.
- A direct answer of 60 to 100 words at the very top.
- Three to five H2 headings, each one answering a sub-question a buyer would ask next.
- One comparison table if the topic involves options.
- A short section on what to ask a supplier or what to check before deciding.
- A short FAQ with three questions.

Rules
- Use only the validated facts above. Do not invent figures, studies or quotes.
- Write short sentences in plain language.
- Replace vague adjectives with figures from the facts list.
- If a fact is missing, write "[fact needed]" and continue.
- Do not mention competitors by name.

Length
600 to 800 words.
```

## After the run

Read every sentence against the claims register. Remove or fix anything that has no source.
