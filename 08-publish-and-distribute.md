# Step 8. Publish and distribute

## Purpose

Put the content where buyers and AI engines can find it, and build signals from other sources. AI answers draw heavily on sources outside the brand's own site, so owned content alone is not enough.

## Inputs

- The approved article and formats
- The checklist results from step 7
- A list of partners, distributors and publications relevant to the topic

## Method

1. Publish the article and note the date. The date is needed to interpret the follow-up.
2. Link the article from related pages and from the main navigation or topic hub.
3. Publish the social, newsletter and video formats on the planned dates.
4. Prepare a short content kit for partners and distributors with the article summary, approved figures and a link. Partners can then use consistent facts on their own pages.
5. Identify third-party sources that AI engines already cite for your prompts. The answer log from step 3 shows them in the `sources_cited` column.
6. Contact those sources for legitimate coverage, such as an expert contribution, a data point or a product listing update.
7. Keep company profiles up to date (LinkedIn, business directories and review platforms where the company is already listed).

## Role of AI

AI drafts the partner kit and outreach messages. A person reviews and sends them.

## Role of the human

A person decides which partners and publications to contact and keeps all statements truthful.

## Output

A publication log with dates, URLs and the list of partner and media actions.

## Quality checks

- Each published item has a date and a URL in the log.
- Partner kits contain only confirmed claims.

## Common mistakes

- Publishing and measuring on the same day.
- Writing reviews or testimonials in the name of people who did not write them.
- Ignoring the sources AI engines already trust for the topic.
