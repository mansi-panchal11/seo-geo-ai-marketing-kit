# Step 1. Select topics

## Purpose

Choose the few subjects where visibility in AI answers will matter most for the business. Tracking everything wastes time and hides the signal.

## Inputs

- A list of products, offers or themes the company wants to grow
- Sales and customer questions
- A list of the main competitors

## Method

1. List 8 to 15 candidate topics. A topic is a subject a buyer asks about, such as "commercial EV charging".
2. Score each topic from 1 to 5 on three criteria.
   - **Business value** how closely the topic links to revenue or a strategic goal.
   - **Real demand** how often buyers ask about it in search engines, AI engines, sales calls or support tickets.
   - **Competitive pressure** how many competitors already appear in answers, or how much market share is at stake.
3. Add the three scores. Keep topics with a total of 11 or more. Drop topics with a total below 7.
4. Choose 3 to 5 topics for the first loop. A small set is easier to measure and improve.
5. Record the decision in `templates/topic-scorecard.csv`.

## Role of AI

AI can suggest candidate topics from a product list and group similar questions. It does not decide which topics matter. A person with business knowledge scores them.

## Output

A scorecard with the chosen topics and the reason for each choice.

## Quality checks

- Each chosen topic links to a product, offer or business goal.
- At least one competitor is known for each topic.
- No topic is chosen only because it sounds interesting.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing topics that are too broad, such as "energy".
- Choosing topics nobody asks about.
- Starting with more than five topics.
